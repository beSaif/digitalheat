## Current plan

### Working idea (stand-in for the missing IDEA)

**Heat is the admission ticket for compute.** Every server-hour needs a "heat permit": room in the house's demand or in its tank. The scheduler issues permits from the weather forecast and fills them with the most urgent job first. **Default: the house does not accept work it has no permits for.** A job that loses its permit is checkpointed and waits if its deadline allows; otherwise it is handed to another site that still has permits. That site is **typed, not named**: a year-round heat user of a kind Leafcloud lists as hosts (indoor pool, care home, hospital; Leafcloud asks for at least 500 GJ/yr of hot-water demand, *sourced*: <https://www.leaf.cloud/leafsites/>). It is outside the reference-house balance (open risk 4). A heated outdoor pool is only a summer sink ("Offers and demand").

- **Novelty claim, narrowed in round 3:** only the house's weather-forecast heat demand plus tank as the capacity curve, refusal at intake, and a published missed-deadline share with accepted share, coverage and dumped heat for one reproducible house-year. Prior art in [research/r3-creativity-usability.md](research/r3-creativity-usability.md) and the [critique](research/r3-creativity-usability-critique.md); round 6 adds ThermAI (round-7 Q&A 6, now in [r8-plan-detail.md](research/r8-plan-detail.md) section A, with its source URL).
- **Pitch form: "heat quote in, heat receipt out".** The creative core is the quote (the admission answer before the job). The receipt (heat delivered after the job) is usability, not novelty: a vendor SDK already reports reused W.h per task.
- **Refuse, not dump (round 8; in the pitch only if it fits, else on the Q&A card, about 35 words):** "Heat is the admission ticket: if the house has no room for the heat, the job is refused at the door, not run with its heat thrown away. The aggregator carries the summer swing, using cloud fallback or a partner site." Do not say "never": the 0 kWh rests on the 2 K guard, and forecast errors can still dump heat. "Aggregator" is the contract's work source, a role: none was found and none is modelled.

### When the model does not pay off

- **Operation only:** below **0.2261 CHF per sold server-hour** (heat credited, server off when unsold); below **0.3325 CHF** if the homeowner gets the heat free (*computed*). At the lowest job rate of the one marketplace with published host rates (at most 0.215 CHF per kW-hour), or LLM work on a CPU-only server at the lowest price (0.148 CHF per server-hour). Worst case, servers left powered while paused: no summer sales, break-even 0.2659 / 0.2814 (*computed*, `analysis/r2_realism.py`).
- **Hardware, price per sold server-hour (*computed*, `r7_payback.py` sections 3–6; round-8 wording per [r8-seller-reality.md](research/r8-seller-reality.md) section (c) with the critic's four edits):** the servers do not pay back in five years below about **2.80 CHF per sold hour at an assumed 60% occupancy** of heat-permitted hours (1.80 at 100%; about 4.90 at 34%, an undefined Akash figure; about 3.40 with 8% cost of capital, an unsaved critic estimate), or if GPU-hour prices fall 10% a year (*assumption*; the central line then never pays back). At 60% occupancy, interruptible GPU capacity for the same work costs about 0.90 to 2.00 CHF (discount ratios transferred, *assumption*) and the same work rents uninterrupted for 2.50; only hyperscaler spot list prices (2.90–4.10) are higher. A rented GPU drawing 70% of its rating delivers 30% less heat, so coverage falls from 73% to about 52% at six servers. Refused work goes to the work source's cloud fallback (2.70 needed with Swiss cloud) or a partner site, never to a dry cooler (dumping would need about 1.60: the price of the principle). At 60% occupancy with unsold hours left off, the servers cover only about half the heat demand instead of about four fifths. Today's always-on host rates (1.35–2.03 CHF) belong to a seller form the house cannot use. On a slide, at most two sentences ("Economics, spoken").
- **Hardware, share of list (N=6, simple payback):** less than about 11–12% (5 y; 11.8% / 12.9% incl. maintenance and card failures, `r7_critic_probes.py` E) or 7–8% (8 y) on the reference machine; about 13–15% on the liquid configuration; LLM-only work priced per job below about 32% / 21% (36% / 26% at 5%). **If ~60,000 audio-hours a day per house of transcription demand does not exist, the LLM-only line applies.**
- **Demand:** when the customer is flat rather than heat-shaped: 53.7% / 60.5% coverage, 0% of summer J1 (`r5_pitch_numbers.py` D5); at half the offer rate coverage is 38.0% / 44.6% (*computed*, `r4_headline.py`).
- **Temperatures (*computed*, `python3 analysis/r5_chain.py`, sections 2–3):** a 55/45 radiator house reaches **at least 64.3% / 73.0%** (servers stop in the 853 h when the return exceeds 40 °C). A 45 °C loop: summer 71.1%, radiator houses lose at least 10.6 points at N=8 (`analysis/r4_tank_layout.py`, section 6).
