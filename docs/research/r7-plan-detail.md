## E. Realism column detail (rounds 5-6; still valid, moved)

**Chain** (assumptions list; round-5 detail in [r6-plan-detail.md](r6-plan-detail.md) section I): coolant ≤ 45 °C in (*assumption*) → about 55 °C out with variable-flow outlet control → CDU, 5 K approach → 1,000 l stratified buffer, top about 50 °C, **valid only while the house return is ≤ 40 °C**. Coolant rise is about 10 K per plate at 600 W and 0.9 l/min (PG25; *computed* by hand). A 50 °C inlet plus 10 K gives 60 °C at the plate outlet, zero margin for Alphacool and EK, so only JetCool-class plates fit. TAVG < 80 °C is NVIDIA's design target for the cooler, not a coolant limit. A GPU about 24–35 K above the fluid at 600 W is *unverified*, so a power cap may be needed. Hot water: fresh-water station to 47 °C, electric cylinder to 55 °C.

- **Leak mitigation (assumptions list only, no build):** a CDU plate exchanger separates the server loop from house water; inhibited coolant, pressure test, dripless quick-disconnects and drip tray; a leak sensor powers the box down (counted as a server failure, already covered by `r5_miss_stress.py`).
