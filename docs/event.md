# sprintd Basel · GreenTech Edition — event info relevant to the DigitalHeat case

Source: <https://app.sprintd.org/events/sprintd-basel-greentech> (copied 2026-11-20)

## Event

- **When:** 20–22 November 2026, doors 17:00 CET Friday
- **Where:** FHNW Campus Dreispitz, Basel
- **Format:** offline, 36-hour sprint, ~200 builders, teams of up to 4
- **Theme:** GreenTech innovation sprint around Basel-Stadt's net-zero-by-2037 goal; runs during Global Entrepreneurship Week Basel
- **Cases:** 2 — DigitalHeat (ours) and ALTYCA (GreenOps for SMEs)
- **Prizes:** CHF 5,000 total. DigitalHeat case prize: dinner with the founders and a chance to keep working on the idea with DigitalHeat (project work or internship).
- Powered by BaselTech, Basel Circular and Basel 2037.
